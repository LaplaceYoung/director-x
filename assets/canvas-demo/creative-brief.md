# Director X local canvas demo

## Creative intent

Show how a video-production Run turns source material into reviewable media. The canvas should make every real file playable or readable and keep its lineage visible.

## Material roles

- The Director X logo is the brand anchor.
- The two WAIC Moss promo posters are image references for their matching video cuts.
- The extracted AAC track represents the editable audio layer used by the revised cut.
- Promo V2 is the first assembled cut.
- Promo V4 is the revised review candidate.

## Review focus

Compare pacing, information density, brand readability, and audio continuity. The revised cut should remain traceable to the brief, brand asset, poster, prior cut, and extracted audio.

## Rights note

All demo files are local project assets already included in this repository. They are used only to exercise the Director X canvas UI and are not presented as third-party stock assets.
