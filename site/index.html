02 / Dedicated crew

A crew,
not a swarm.

Director X uses focused production roles with explicit ownership, artifacts, permissions, and handoffs. Independent work can run concurrently without becoming anonymous.

03 / Live canvas

The canvas shows the work.
Not just the workflow.

References, research, scripts, images, audio, shots, cuts, approvals, and review evidence appear as they are created. Their relationships remain visible throughout the task.

Reference→Analysis→Script→Keyframe→Clip→Cut→Review

Not a static flowchart. A live map of what exists, where it came from, and what it becomes next.
