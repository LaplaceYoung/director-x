# Avatar Lipsync Guide

## Identity Stability

- Record hair, face shape, outfit, accessories, and posture as memory keys.
- Keep background simple when lip sync is the main risk.
- Avoid generated text in the avatar frame.

## Voice And Sync

- Segment long scripts into natural breath groups.
- Align subtitle timing to speech, not visual cuts.
- Review consonant-heavy lines and fast numbers carefully.

## Safety

- Use user-owned or consented likenesses.
- Label synthetic presenter workflows when required by platform or policy.
- Keep sensitive advice sourced and qualified.
