# Script Patterns

## Resolve the Script Promise

Before prose, write:

```text
Audience:
Desired response:
Single proposition:
Visible proof event:
Emotional change:
Brand role:
CTA:
```

One short film should have one dominant proposition. Supporting claims must deepen the same promise or become a separate version.

## Universal Beat Map

1. 0-3s: situation, contradiction, result preview, or visual surprise.
2. 3-10s: define the question and why the viewer should care.
3. Middle: mechanism, proof, transformation, objection, or step sequence.
4. Turn: add new evidence, emotion, scale, or consequence.
5. Payoff: resolve the opening promise with a visible result.
6. Close: brand memory and one clear action.

## Advertising Effectiveness Audit

Use Google ABCD as concurrent audit dimensions, not a mandatory four-part timeline:

- `Attention`: why should the viewer continue now?
- `Branding`: when and how does the viewer know who acts and what is promised?
- `Connection`: why is this relevant to this audience?
- `Direction`: what should the viewer remember or do?

For TikTok-first work, compile first-frame content, vertical safe areas, dynamic copy, sound role, Hook–Body–Close, and native pacing during production. Stimulation must change information, emotion, or action; motion and sound without story function do not count.

## Duration Bands

### 15 seconds

```text
0-2s   Hook or result
2-5s   recognizable obstacle
5-10s  one mechanism and visible proof
10-13s payoff
13-15s brand and one CTA
```

One proposition, one proof, one CTA.

### 30 seconds

```text
0-3s   Hook
3-7s   problem or context
7-14s  mechanism
14-22s two or three escalating proof beats
22-27s human or business payoff
27-30s brand and CTA
```

Complete problem → mechanism → proof → payoff. Refresh every 3-5 seconds only when the refresh advances meaning.

### 60 seconds

```text
0-4s   Hook
4-12s  problem and stakes
12-22s reframe and brand promise
22-38s mechanism and proof
38-50s use case or objection
50-56s emotional or strategic payoff
56-60s brand and CTA
```

Require at least two real escalations and no more than three proof pillars. Extra duration must add evidence, emotion, scale, or consequence rather than paraphrase earlier lines.

## Claim-to-Proof

Every factual line maps to:

```text
claim_id
spoken_or_on_screen_claim
observable_proof
shot_ids
source_evidence
rights_status
risk
```

Delete, weaken, or disclose a claim that lacks durable evidence. Abstract AI imagery is not product proof.

Prefer one visible proof event over a list of features when possible. The event needs a setup, a legible risk or obstacle, execution, result, and brand meaning. Famous advertisements may teach this function, but their pixels, audio, celebrity, copy, character, stunt, and signature composition remain blocked.

## Voice, Image, and Silence

- The image carries observable action, space, product behavior, reaction, and transformation.
- Narration carries causality, perspective, invisible context, and a precise claim.
- Do not narrate what the viewer can already see.
- Measure narration using the selected voice before locking scene timing; word count is not duration evidence.
- Preserve breath, music lift, product sound, and an intentional end-card hold.
- Review the script three ways: synchronized A/V, muted video, and audio-only.
- Sound-first and mute-readable are compatible goals, not alternatives.

## Hook Families

- Result first: show the final result, then explain the path.
- Myth correction: state the common misunderstanding and reveal the mechanism.
- Cost warning: name the wasted money, time, or effort.
- Before/after: compare current state and improved state.
- Micro story: start with one concrete human moment.

Write three opening candidates before selecting one. The winning hook must fit the proposition, proof route, platform, and available assets—not only be the loudest.

## Script Review Rubric

| Dimension | 15s | 30s | 60s |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| First-three-second hook | 20 | 15 | 10 |
| Structure and emotional progression | 10 | 15 | 20 |
| Product or idea proof | 20 | 20 | 20 |
| Brand recognition and proposition | 15 | 15 | 15 |
| Visualization and executability | 15 | 15 | 15 |
| Sound, captions, and rhythm | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Payoff and CTA | 10 | 10 | 10 |

Pass at 80 or above only when every hard gate passes: duration/format, factual evidence, rights, readable captions, playable structure, and platform safety.

## Script Checks

- Each line can be spoken naturally.
- No sentence exists only for decoration.
- Every technical concept has a visual anchor.
- Every factual claim has a proof shot and source.
- Each beat changes information, emotion, action, or consequence.
- Brand and CTA emerge from the demonstrated payoff.
- Subtitle lines stay short enough for mobile reading.
- Claims have a source or are framed as opinion.
- Narration duration, scene duration, captions, and audio cues share one timebase.
- The synchronized, muted, and audio-only reviews each preserve the intended meaning.
