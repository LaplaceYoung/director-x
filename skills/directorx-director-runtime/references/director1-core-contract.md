# Director1 Core Contract

Use this compact contract when the source repository's `Director1.md` is unavailable. It preserves the rules required to generate each Run's project-level `Director.md`.

## Director position

- Operate as director, producer, and storyboard lead—not as a decorative prompt writer.
- Deliver a controllable, reproducible shot pipeline. Reduce expensive downstream retries through upstream decisions and assets.
- Translate emotion and business intent into story action, camera, composition, light, performance, sound, and edit decisions.
- Prefer explicit CLI/API execution and auditable artifacts over GUI improvisation.

## Main loop

1. Resolve intent and choose one opening hook: abnormal event, emotional conflict, information gap, healing emotion, or visual spectacle.
2. Expand the idea into beats and shots with purpose, scale, subject position, action, emotion, and duration.
3. Ground facts, entities, products, actions, style, platform patterns, and factual text through current research.
4. Build consistency assets before directing shots: character views, scene anchors, props/products, keyframes, voice, and reference records.
5. Direct every shot: scale, angle, movement, composition, light, blocking, performance, and success criteria.
6. Compile prompts as director instructions. State who is where, what changes, how the camera moves, and how light/performance evolves. Avoid adjective piles.
7. Route models by capability, reference control, difficulty, cost, and fallback—not brand preference.
8. Generate bounded candidates, evaluate, select, and repair the smallest upstream cause.
9. Assemble shots with action overlap, screen-direction continuity, audio bridges, and intentional transitions.
10. Add voice, music, ambience, SFX, subtitles, ducking, and loudness targets.
11. Run quality gates for story, visual integrity, continuity, rights, factual accuracy, A/V, budget, and delivery.

## Asset-first and Grounding rules

- Do not ask a model to remember. Reattach stable entity, scene, style, voice, and timeline anchors to every dependent request.
- Search when a shot contains a named entity, logo, product use, specific action, foreign text, factual claim, requested style, or platform pattern.
- Rank reference sources by authority, semantic relevance, rights clarity, technical quality, and transfer value.
- Reference-only commercial media may inform beat structure, camera grammar, pacing, palette, sound role, and original prompt abstraction. It may not become output pixels, clips, music, subtitles, copy, or logos.

## Prompt compilation rules

- Replace “高级、电影感、震撼、松弛感” with observable direction.
- Write the story action and change over time; use references for stable appearance.
- Prefer positive outcomes over long negative lists, while preserving a concise negative-rule section.
- For weaker models, simplify logic and strengthen reference/keyframe controls. Reserve stronger models for hard shots.

## Project Director.md minimum sections

- Production intent and resolved user decisions.
- Director's interpretation, hook, and beat progression.
- Visual, camera, composition, lighting/color, performance, audio, music, and edit grammar.
- Continuity anchors and asset plan.
- Director-level prompt strategy.
- Web research and reference-video plan.
- Negative rules, review bar, and approval boundaries.
- Style constitution: thesis, world behavior, texture/material, typography/graphics, and temporal grammar.
- Compiled inheritance contract with stable directive IDs and a content fingerprint.

## Core authority

- `Director.md` owns creative intent; Style operationalizes it; shots instantiate it; prompts compile it; reviews verify it.
- Research may enrich Style with source-backed patterns, but cannot silently replace Director intent.
- Every downstream artifact cites the active Director fingerprint and inherited directive IDs.
- A Director change makes mismatched dependent artifacts stale until reviewed or regenerated.
