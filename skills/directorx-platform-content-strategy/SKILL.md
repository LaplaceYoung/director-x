# DirectorX Platform Content Strategy

Use this skill when adapting a video brief to platform behavior, duration, audience habits, hook density, publishing package, script structure, or cross-platform derivatives.

## Workflow

1. Identify platform, duration, content type, audience, creator voice, and publish goal.
2. Read `references/platform-playbook.md` for platform and script rules.
3. Convert user intent into a `platform_publishing_profile` and `script_retention_plan`.
4. Specify hook, beat map, subtitle style, cover/title idea, interaction prompt, and derivative plan.
5. Feed visual and audio requirements to Director Runtime as capability-neutral production instructions.

## Output Requirements

- Platform profile and rationale.
- Duration and pacing rule.
- First 1-3 seconds hook.
- Beat-by-beat script plan.
- Subtitle and cover/title direction.
- Interaction prompt.
- Risk notes for platform fit.
- Cross-platform derivative edits.

## Quality Bar

- Every beat must earn retention, clarity, or conversion.
- Platform habits must shape pipeline, script, subtitle, cover, audio, and publishing package.
- Script rules must remain executable by `Director.md`, provider routing, Remotion, audio, and final review.
