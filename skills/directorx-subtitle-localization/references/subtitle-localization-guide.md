# Subtitle Localization Guide

## Chinese Mobile Segmentation

- Prefer one idea per subtitle segment.
- Keep line breaks aligned with speech rhythm.
- Avoid dense punctuation clusters.
- Use Arabic numerals for measurable claims when clarity improves.
- Keep technical terms consistent across title, subtitles, and description.

## Platform Rules

| Platform | Subtitle Style |
| --- | --- |
| Douyin | Dense, high contrast, key phrases highlighted, strong lower safe-area control. |
| Kuaishou | Clear and conversational, moderate emphasis, practical phrasing. |
| Bilibili | Accurate terms, optional chapter labels, lower visual urgency. |
| Xiaohongshu | Cover-title consistency, save-friendly list phrasing. |
| WeChat Channels | Clean, trustworthy, easy to share in muted autoplay. |

## QA

- Subtitles never cover product proof or face details.
- Every spoken claim has matching text or visual support.
- Cover title and first subtitle do not repeat the same words mechanically.
