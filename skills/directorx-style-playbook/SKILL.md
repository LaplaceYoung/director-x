# Director X Style Playbook

Use this skill after `Director.md` exists and research has produced source-backed transferable patterns.

## Workflow

1. Read `Director.md`, `director_contract.json`, `intent_resolution.json`, and `references/style-playbook-contract.md`.
2. Extract a one-sentence style thesis and observable rules for world behavior, image, camera, composition, light/color, material, performance, typography, motion, edit rhythm, voice, music, SFX, subtitles, and negative space.
3. Cite the active Director fingerprint and inherited directive IDs. Never create a parallel creative brief.
4. Turn research into original production rules; preserve source, evidence, transfer target, rights boundary, and confidence.
5. Write rules as `condition → direction → evidence → failure → repair`. Avoid adjectives that cannot be reviewed.
6. Define shot-level inheritance and explicit override records.
7. Define evaluation dimensions for prompt adherence, visual quality, temporal transition completion, human/object fidelity, physics, commonsense, world consistency, edit fit, audio continuity, and rights.
8. After accepted candidates or final review, append only proven lessons to `style_learning_log.json`; promote a lesson into the Style Bible only after repeated evidence or explicit director approval.

## Required Outputs

- `style_playbook.json`
- `style_learning_log.json`
- Director binding with `fingerprint`, `inherited_directive_ids`, and `override_records`
- Source-backed pattern records with rights-safe transfer notes
- Machine-reviewable evaluation and repair rules

## Quality Gate

- Every rule is observable and testable.
- Camera movement has a narrative or information function.
- Stable appearance is carried by assets; prompts describe change over time.
- J/L cuts and audio bridges are explicit timeline decisions, not generic “smooth transitions.”
- Generated-video review includes temporal and intrinsic world fidelity, not aesthetics alone.
- No Style rule contradicts Director.md without a recorded, approved override.

## Reference

Read `references/style-playbook-contract.md` completely before writing or reviewing a Style Bible.
