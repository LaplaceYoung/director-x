# Production Review Rubric

Score each candidate from 0 to 1.

## Shot Quality

- Subject clarity.
- Motion smoothness.
- Action completeness.
- Visual artifacts.
- Provider-specific constraints.
- World/geometry consistency.
- Action completeness.

## Continuity

- Entity consistency.
- Scene consistency.
- Style consistency.
- Voice/music consistency.
- Timeline logic.

## Edit Fit

- Shot purpose fulfilled.
- Preceding and following shot compatibility.
- Rhythm fit.
- Audio/subtitle alignment.
- Story or conversion role.

## Evidence Contract

- Every video review includes at least one timecoded evidence frame or interval.
- Each evidence item names the evaluated dimension and concrete observation.
- Defects include severity, timecode, description, and smallest repair action.
- Accept only when the mean reaches the shot threshold and every critical dimension is at least `0.5`.
- Audio-bearing video additionally records audio/visual synchronization; do not average an A/V sync failure into visual quality.

## Cost Decision

- Continue when quality is below threshold and budget remains.
- Accept when quality meets threshold.
- Reroute when failure matches provider weakness.
- Simplify when the shot design exceeds budget.
- Request approval when delivery promise changes.
