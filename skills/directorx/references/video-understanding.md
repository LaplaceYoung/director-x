# Video understanding

Use FFprobe and FFmpeg as evidence preparation for Codex, not as a substitute for visual judgment.

1. Preserve the original source and provenance.
2. Separate audio at 48 kHz.
3. Extract all frames for short reference films. For long material, build a complete proxy-frame index and inspect original-resolution frames for selected ranges.
4. Build contact sheets and scene-change keyframes.
5. Inspect each shot for:
   - time range and duration
   - narrative purpose
   - shot size and composition
   - camera and subject motion
   - lighting, contrast, color, texture
   - typography and UI
   - transition and edit rhythm
   - dialogue, music, effects, silence, and beat sync
6. Separate transferable technique from protected expression. Recreate principles, not source pixels, brand assets, performances, or copy.
7. Put the reference, audio, keyframes, palette, shot analysis, visual system, and original remake plan on the canvas.
